# Candidate scorecard

Used by: [CV Screener](../employees/hiring/02-cv-screener.md), [Candidate Shortlist Ranker](../employees/hiring/03-candidate-shortlist-ranker.md), [Interview Question Builder](../employees/hiring/04-interview-question-builder.md), [Job Post Writer](../employees/hiring/01-job-post-writer.md).

One scorecard per role. Fill it in before the first CV arrives and do not change it mid way, or old and new scores stop being comparable. Copy this file, rename it for the role, and give the copy to your AI employees.

## How to fill it in (10 minutes)

1. Write the 3 to 5 results this person must deliver in their first 90 days.
2. For each result, ask: which skill or experience shows they can do it? That becomes a criterion.
3. Mark each criterion MUST HAVE (needed on day 1) or NICE TO HAVE. Keep must haves to 5 or fewer.
4. Write what 0, 1, 2 and 3 look like on a CV for each criterion. Be concrete, so two people would give the same score.
5. Set the tie break order: which criteria win when totals are equal.

Labels from the total: STRONG = at least 80 percent of the maximum, MAYBE = 50 to 79 percent, WEAK = below 50 percent. Change these if you like, but write the new numbers here.

## Never scored, never recorded

Name, age, date of birth, gender, photo, nationality, ethnicity, religion, marital or family status, health or disability, and any other detail protected where you hire. Employment gaps are not scored either: the criteria decide, not a guess about why.
