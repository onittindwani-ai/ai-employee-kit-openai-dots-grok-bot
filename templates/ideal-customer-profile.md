# Ideal customer profile (ICP) sheet

Used by: [LinkedIn Lead Finder](../employees/sales/01-linkedin-lead-finder.md), [Lead Enricher and Qualifier](../employees/sales/02-lead-enricher-and-qualifier.md), [Cold Email First Drafts](../employees/sales/03-cold-email-first-drafts.md), [Lost Deal Win Back](../employees/sales/09-lost-deal-win-back.md).

Your ideal customer profile is a short description of the companies and people who get the most value from you and are easiest to win. Your AI employees use it to decide who to look for and how to score them. A vague profile gives a vague list, so be specific.

## How to fill it in (20 minutes)

1. List your 3 to 5 best clients ever (best = paid well, got results, easy to work with). Keep this list private; do not give client names to the AI unless you are comfortable with that.
2. Write down what they had in common. That becomes the profile.
3. Write the buying signals: things you can see in public that suggest a company needs you now.
4. Write the deal breakers: things that mean "not a fit", even if the rest matches.
5. Set the scoring rules so the AI scores the same way you would.

## Scoring rules

| Score | Meaning | Example rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8 to 10 (HOT) | Strong fit and a buying signal | Matches industry, size and title, plus at least 1 signal |
| 5 to 7 (WARM) | Good fit, no signal yet | Matches at least 2 of industry, size and title |
| 1 to 4 (NOT A FIT) | Weak fit or a deal breaker | Matches 1 or fewer, or any deal breaker |
